Designed: March 2026
Duration: 12 weeks (3 mesocycles of 4 weeks)
Session length: 55-70 min
Structure: Push/Pull/Legs/Push/Pull

Mon → Push A (Chest focus)
Tue → Pull A (Width focus)
Wed → Legs
Thu → Push B (Shoulder focus)
Fri → Pull B (Thickness focus)

⚙️ PROGRESSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Double progression:
→ Hit top of rep range on ALL sets
→ Add 2.5 kg (upper) / 5 kg (lower)
→ Reset to bottom of range, repeat

RIR by week:
Wk 1: RIR 3-4 (learn)
Wk 2: RIR 2-3 (build)
Wk 3: RIR 1-2 (push)
Wk 4: DELOAD (half sets, same weight)

Repeat x3 mesocycles = 12 weeks

🔑 KEY EXERCISE NOTES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lengthened-position exercises (priority):
• Incline DB Curl — arms behind torso for max biceps stretch
• Bayesian Cable Curl — face away from cable, arm behind body
• Overhead Triceps Ext — deep stretch on long head
• Skull Crushers — load triceps long head at stretch
• Cable Lateral Raise behind body — side delt at longest length
• DB Fly — deep chest stretch at bottom
• Dips — deep pec stretch at bottom
• RDL — hamstrings loaded at longest length
• Seated Leg Curl — longer hamstring length than lying
• Standing Calf Raise — gastrocnemius at full stretch

Equipment needed:
Barbell + plates, dumbbells, cable station,
pull-up bar, dip bars, hack squat or leg press,
leg curl machine, leg extension machine,
reverse pec deck, preacher curl bench,
EZ-bar (for skull crushers)

No Smith machine required.
